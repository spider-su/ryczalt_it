# Ryczałt IT

**Short description:** Look up Polish company details by NIP and configure KSeF access.

**Full description:**

Ryczałt IT helps Polish sole proprietors validate their NIP and look up business details in the official VAT taxpayer register.

Save your company profile locally, securely store your KSeF token, check authentication, and remove stored credentials when needed.

The free mode requires no Ryczałt IT account or proprietary backend. Internet access is needed for government services.

Invoice synchronization and PIT/VAT/ZUS calculations are not available in this version. KSeF authentication checks do not verify invoice-read permissions.

This is not an official Ministry of Finance app and does not submit tax returns or replace accounting advice.
